Read before using the data

Schedules use the final run's 2026 calendar, starting on Thursday. The saved Honeybee parameters say Sunday; downstream measures set the effective calendar to Thursday. Times label the start of each 15-minute interval in local standard time. No separate holiday calendar is supplied and the display does not apply daylight saving adjustments.

Occupancy schedules multiply the reference People value. Lighting and equipment schedules multiply their load densities. Heating and cooling schedules use °C. Consult the schedule unit and native definition for other values.

Outdoor-air entries are HBJSON zone inputs. They do not establish final ERV supply/extract quantities, diversity or heat-pump equipment capacities. The Annual energy page records the downstream shared ERV and window/heat-pump controls from the reviewed run.
