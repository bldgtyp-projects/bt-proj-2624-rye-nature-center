Using the data

Schedules use a 2026 calendar starting on a Thursday. Times mark the start of each 15-minute interval, in local standard time with no daylight saving and no holidays. The saved Honeybee parameters list Sunday as the start day; the simulation runs on Thursday.

Occupancy schedules multiply Reference People; lighting and equipment schedules multiply their load densities. Setpoint schedules are in °C and activity in W per person.

Zone outdoor-air values are model inputs, not ERV design airflows or heat-pump capacities. The ERV and controls are on the Annual energy page.
