Rye Nature Center

Discovery Center energy model

EnergyPlus simulations of the Discovery Center, built from the documents Allen + Killcoyne Architects provided on 30 September 2026. The results cover annual energy use, peak heating and cooling loads, and resilience through seven-day winter and summer power outages. The program and model inputs behind them, and the data, are here too.

Prepared for design coordination with Allen + Killcoyne Architects and Kendall Engineering. The heat pumps and ERV in the model are allowances, not equipment selections.
